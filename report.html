1. Exploratory Data Analysis & Seasonality

Commercial aviation passengers show dramatic seasonal patterns. Volume peaks significantly in the summer (June, July, August) and during winter holidays (December), and plummets in January and February.

Core Correlations Heatmap

Analytical Findings

  • Flights and Capacity (ASM) share a near-perfect correlation (~0.99), indicating airlines scale capacity by scheduling flights rather than increasing passenger density.
  • Passengers and RPM are perfectly correlated, showing that Revenue Passenger Miles reflect actual passenger volume scaled by average flight distances.
  • Load Factor (LF) has a moderate correlation with Passengers, proving that higher demand periods naturally drive better seat occupancy (efficiency).

2. Airline Performance Classification

A Decision Tree Classifier was trained to predict whether an airline/airport month operates at an efficient load factor (Load Factor ≥ 78%) based on passenger volume, scheduled flights, and passenger density.

Mitigation Action Plan

Capacity 1. Seasonal Capacity Realignment

Action: Prune flight frequencies by 10-15% during structural troughs (January and February) for underperforming airlines. Reallocate aircraft to high-performing summer hubs to protect the Load Factor.

Pricing 2. Seasonal Dynamic Pricing Strategies

Action: Implement aggressive promotional pricing and low-tier leisure fares during off-peak autumn and winter months to stimulate demand, driving load factors back above the 78% efficiency line.
